## Past

You lie beneath the boughs of the Great Oak, your hair tangled with wildflowers. The world hums — bees, wind, distant songbirds — and then… silence.

The air ripples. Light filters down through the canopy, turning gold. The forest around you melts into sunlight, the trees stretching and bending until they become towers — slender, elegant, alive.

A city of gold and green rises before you, its streets paved with petals, its spires reaching for the sun. Elves walk there, radiant and ageless, their laughter like music you somehow know.

One of them turns — a woman with eyes like your own.

“Come home,” she whispers. Her voice is both thunder and lullaby.

You reach out — but the vision dissolves into motes of pollen and starlight. The whisper lingers, Come home.

The sand shifts, and the gold turns to ash.

## Future

Smoke chokes the horizon. The forest — your forest — is burning. The Great Oak groans as fire licks its bark. You stand among the flames, unable to move.

Through the smoke, you see her again — the golden woman. This time, she stands not in light, but in shadow. Behind her, the same golden city shines faintly through the haze.

Forest is on fire and the tree talks to Fara-Quill - you must choose whether to stay or go during this harsh time of the fire

The vision ends with a single leaf drifting down through the smoke — half green, half gold — and turning to dust before it hits the ground.
