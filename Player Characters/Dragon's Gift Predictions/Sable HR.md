## Past

You wait in silence. The men are gone.

The world feels distant — hollow — except for the pulse in your ears and the warmth spilling from your stomach.

Then — footsteps. Light ones. Familiar.

“Sable!”

She runs to you before you can stop her, her hands glowing with the soft shimmer of healing magic. You feel her trying to piece you back together. You feel the sting of hope — and then the sound.

The arrow lands.

A gasp, a stumble, and she’s falling into your arms. The light fades from her hands, and the world narrows to the sound of your own heartbeat.

Hours pass — or seconds. You carry her away when the streets are silent again. Her blood stains your hands, and her last words haunt the night:

“Don’t lose yourself.”


## Future


You blink — and the world is burning.

A tavern you half-recognize crackles with flame, wooden beams collapsing around you. The smoke claws at your throat. Shadows move — panicked figures — but everything is blurred, indistinct.

You see a shape through the firelight — a figure you know.

Their face is obscured by the smoke, but their stance, their voice — it’s them. Someone you traveled with, lived life with.

For a heartbeat, it’s Selkie again.

They reach toward you, and the timbers groan. A burst of flame erupts between you. You rush forward — but the ceiling gives way.

The moment freezes.

You’re holding someone again — weight in your arms, heat on your skin, the scent of ash and iron. The world flickers between then and now. Between who you lost — and who you might lose.

And then — silence. Only the fire’s slow hiss, and the echo of a promise you never could keep.

Some fires never die. Some moments burn forever.
