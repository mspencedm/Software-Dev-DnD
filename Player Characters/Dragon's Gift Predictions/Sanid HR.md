## Past

The rhythmic clang of the forge echoes through the night — a heartbeat of fire and metal. Sparks rain down as Sanid shapes the final edge of a blade meant for a neighboring guard.
Outside, laughter drifts through the open door — her wife’s laughter.

Aria’s voice calls, warm and teasing.

“You’ll miss supper again if you keep romancing that hammer.”

Sanid smirks, wiping soot from her cheek as she turns — but the laughter fades. The air grows still.
The scent of smoke no longer comes from her forge. It rolls in thick, black, choking.

She bursts from the doorway — their home engulfed. Shapes in the fire. Steel flashing in the dark.
She fights, roars, bleeds — until the world narrows to silence and the sight of Aria’s lifeless hand reaching from the cinders, still clutching the small carving of a sun they’d made for the child yet to come.

Ash settles like snow.
Sanid falls to her knees — the fire dying in her forge, and in her heart.



## Future

Ashes swirl again — but this time, they rise. The ground trembles under her boots.
The ruin around her is unrecognizable — not the forge, not her home. Just blackened earth and dying flame.

A figure begins to form from the dust — crawling, trembling, fragile.
Small hands, a face half-formed from soot and shadow. Eyes the same grey-green as hers.

It looks at her with confusion… with pain.

“...Mother?”

The voice is young. Fractured. Wrong.
A whisper follows, distant and distorted, from somewhere unseen —

“Save me.”

Then the ash collapses inward, the world snapping back to stillness.
All that remains is the echo of the voice and the heat of the forge long gone cold.
