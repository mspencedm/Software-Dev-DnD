## Past

🌙 Evaluna Past Vision – “The Bodyguard Assignment”

Silver sand swirls and glimmers in the Hourglass, catching the soft moonlight. Maelis murmurs,
“The past remembers those who stood closest, even when unseen.”

You are young again, a hundred years old in elven terms, standing in the grand Starwhisper Manor gardens. Birds flutter between flowering trees, and moonlight glances off the marble columns.

A figure approaches: Sven, assigned as your personal bodyguard. He is young, competent, yet formal — the weight of responsibility already clear in his posture. His eyes sweep over the gardens, the halls, always alert.

“Mistress Evaluna,” he says, bowing slightly. “I will ensure your safety.”

You blink at him, a mixture of curiosity and amusement bubbling up. You are not used to someone following so closely — and yet, there’s a quiet steadiness in him. You notice how he studies your movements, anticipating danger even where none seems to exist.

As the day progresses, you wander through the garden, mischievous and whimsical, and Sven keeps pace — silent, patient. A fox darts across the path; you laugh and chase it, Sven’s eyes following you with a mixture of exasperation and care.

In this moment, a bond begins to form.

The vision fades with a final glance: Sven standing just behind you, a protective shadow, the faintest smile tugging at the corner of his mouth — a promise that he will always be there, even if you cannot yet see why.



## Future


The silver and gold sand swirls in the Hourglass, catching faint moonlight. Maelis murmurs:
“The future remembers not just the familiar faces, but the weight of the places they once called home.”

You are standing at what was once Starwhisper Manor, but it is almost unrecognizable. Marble columns lie shattered, gardens are overgrown and tangled, and walls are cracked, blackened in places as if the moonlight itself mourns. The air smells of earth and dust, with a faint trace of smoke.

From the far end of the ruins, a figure moves quickly — Sven. He runs with purpose, almost as if trying to save you, though it is hard to tell from this distance. His expression is tight, urgency etched into every line of his face. The familiar steadiness of the past is now layered with tension, worry, and fear.

“Evaluna!” he calls, voice echoing across the broken halls.

You see him dart between collapsed walls, leap over toppled stone, a shadow of his younger self but carrying the same protective instinct. Moonlight glints off his armor, dust clinging to him as he presses forward.

The ruins seem to be collapsing in on you as Sven rushes towards you. Then, all goes black.
