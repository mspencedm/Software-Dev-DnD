# 🩸 The Blood Moon Ambush
> *A short one-shot encounter for a traveling party — perfect for a mid-journey diversion or an eerie night attack.*

---

### **Adventure Background**
The Blood Moon rises once every century — a harbinger of old wars and restless souls.  
This night, the adventurers’ caravan creaks along a fog-choked forest road. The horses grow uneasy as spectral hoofbeats echo in the distance.  
Without warning, crimson mist spills from the trees — the spirits of fallen knights rise again to claim the living.

---

### **Setup**
- The caravan has **20 wagons**, a handful of **NPC merchants**, and the party escorting them.  
- The fog heavily obscures sight beyond **30 ft**.  
- The first ambush wave begins before anyone can rest or prepare spells.  
- The road is **narrow**, flanked by gnarled trees — perfect for undead to ride through and circle around.

---

### **Hook**
Rumors tell of a **Headless Horseman** haunting the Blood Road, searching for his lost skull.  
The merchants dismiss it as a tale… until they hear the galloping.  

---

### **Combat Encounter**
The ambush happens in escalating waves.  
Each wave grows in intensity as the dead reclaim the road.

---

### **🪦 Combat Flow**
- **Round 1:** Haunted Soldiers (1–2) and a Cursed Bowman strike first from the fog.  
- **Round 2:** The **Headless Horseman** charges through the mist, followed by two more soldiers.  
- **Round 3:** The **Bloodshade Specter** materializes above the caravan, shrieking vengeance.
